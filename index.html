Is my color up yet?

I surveyed 32 hackathon attendees about food waves and found the gap wasn't on Discord. It was in the room. So I designed one shared status for the phone and the screen above the food area, and built it as a working prototype.

About

I'm a UX design student at Western Governors University and a Lead Early Learning Tutor. I like problems I've run into myself, because I can see them up close before I research them properly.

My projects follow the same path: notice a real problem, investigate it with real people, let the evidence change my mind, then design and build something that can be tested.
